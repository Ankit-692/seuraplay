# Privacy Policy for SeuraPlay

Last updated: September 16, 2026

SeuraPlay is an open-source application. This policy outlines how your data is handled.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
SeuraPlay is designed with privacy in mind:
- **Local Data**: All your playlists, history, and application settings are stored locally on your device.
- **No Third-Party Tracking**: We do not use third-party analytics, tracking libraries, or advertising networks.

## 2. Google Drive Access
SeuraPlay requests access to Google Drive using the restricted application data scope (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata`):
- **Purpose**: This permission is used exclusively to allow you to back up and restore your local database and settings to your personal Google Drive account.
- **Access Limits**: The app can **only** read and write files created by SeuraPlay inside its dedicated hidden application folder. It **cannot** view, modify, or delete any of your personal files, documents, or photos in Google Drive.
- **Sharing**: Your backup data is stored directly in your personal Google account. The developers of SeuraPlay never have access to your data or credentials.

## 3. Changes to This Policy
Because SeuraPlay is open source, any future changes to this policy will be committed directly to this repository.
